Outerwear Craftsmanship

The Architecture of Heavyweight Outerwear

Stationed in Soho, Manhattan, BoldJacketWay was established to champion full-grain steerhide leather, double-breasted trench gabardine, and heavyweight Melton wool tailoring through rigorous empirical research.

Atelier Mandate

Heirloom Tailoring Over Fast Fashion Outerwear

In an era dominated by thin bonded synthetic leathers and flimsy polyester coats, BoldJacketWay advocates for 1.4mm uncorrected steerhide, 800g Melton wool, and horsehair chest canvas tailoring.

Our team of master cutters, leather artisans, and textile engineers evaluate outerwear through practical stress testing: measuring tensile tear resistance, waterproof hydrostatic head ratings, shoulder arm mobility, and zipper hardware durability.

"True outerwear is wearable armor—built to weather severe elements while developing a rich, personalized patina across decades."

Outerwear Principles

Our Four Craft Standards

1. Full-Grain Steerhide

Mandating 1.2mm to 1.4mm uncorrected full-grain leather for lifetime abrasion protection.

2. Heavyweight Wool

Requiring minimum 700g to 800g felted Melton wool weaves for arctic cold insulation.

3. Heavy Hardware

Demanding solid antique brass zippers, hand-set rivets, and real buffalo horn buttons.

4. Objective Testing

100% independent outerwear evaluations backed by hydrostatic pressure tests and drape audits.
